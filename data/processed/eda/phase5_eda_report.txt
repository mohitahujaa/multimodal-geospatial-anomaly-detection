7. SEASONAL-STRUCTURE FINDINGS & METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Observed Intra-Annual Cycles:
  1. Hot Pre-Monsoon Summer (April - June): Max temperatures (31.3C), high wind, lower vegetative greenness.
  2. Monsoon Season (July - September): Intense precipitation peak, cloud cover, dramatic vegetation green-up.
  3. Post-Monsoon / Early Winter (October - November): Max NDVI peak (0.35), falling temperatures, stable lighting.
  4. Winter Fog / Inversion Period (December - January): Minimum temperatures (12.0C), optical atmospheric attenuation.
CRITICAL METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATION:
  - The current panel contains only ONE observation per calendar month (12 months total).
  - This single annual cycle CANNOT establish a statistically robust multi-year climatological baseline.
  - It is mathematically impossible from 12 months to distinguish an anomalous meteorological year from the true historical mean.

Baseline Trade-off Analysis:
  - Baseline A (Cell Temporal Mean): Captures cell spatial intercept perfectly; residual std is minimal for stable features.
  - Baseline B (Cell Temporal Median): Robust to single-month extreme spikes; slightly higher MAE on normal features.
  - Baseline C (Causal Lagged Rolling Mean): Strictly causal (no leakage), but suffers high MAE during sharp seasonal transitions
    (e.g., lagging behind monsoon green-up or winter cooling) and has fewer evaluation observations (month 0 is NaN).
  - Baseline D (Robust Median + MAD Scaling): Superior for anomaly scoring because MAD prevents variance inflation from spikes,
    while division by zero is safely guarded (MAD=0 sets robust_z=0).

11. DATA STANDARDIZATION & SCALING RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Canonical Dataset Status: Kept 100% untouched in original physical units.
Recommendations for Future Feature Transformations in Phase 6:
  1. VIIRS Radiance: Right-skewed distribution. Candidate log1p(x) transformation compresses extreme industrial outliers
     and stabilizes variance.
  2. Optical Indices (NDVI, NDBI, NDWI): Naturally bounded within [-1, 1]. Robust standard scaling (x - median) / (1.4826 * MAD)
     is recommended over standard z-score to avoid skew from cloud contamination.
  3. Weather Variables: Extreme seasonal ranges (Temp: 12C to 31C; Precip: 1 to 283 mm). Raw physical values should not be fed
     directly to distance-based detectors without seasonal centering.

13. METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS OF 12-MONTH DATASET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Single Seasonal Realization: With only one observation per calendar month, normal seasonality is completely confounded
   with inter-annual variation. We cannot know if March 2024 was abnormally warm or typical.
2. Cloud Sensitivity: Single cloudy months (July and September 2023) eliminate 25-30% of optical observations for that epoch.
3. Trend Indistinguishability: Cannot separate long-term urban development trends from temporary seasonal fluctuations.

14. RECOMMENDATION FOR HISTORICAL DATA EXPANSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EVIDENCE-BASED RECOMMENDATION:
  Expand historical dataset to at least 24 to 36 consecutive months (e.g., 2022-04 to 2025-03).
  Justification:
    a) 2-3 observations per calendar month allows establishing true seasonal climatological medians and MADs.
    b) Eliminates single-monsoon data gaps: if July 2023 is cloudy, July 2022 and July 2024 provide baseline reference.
    c) Enables robust calculation of Seasonal Z-Scores: z_{i, m, y} = (x_{i, m, y} - mu_{i, m}) / sigma_{i, m}.

15. ARCHITECTURAL SEPARATION: HISTORICAL BASELINE VS. CURRENT OPERATIONAL DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dual-Horizon System Architecture:
  - Historical Baseline Tier (2022–2024 / 2023–2025): Dedicated multi-year training panel used strictly to compute
    cell-level climatological distributions, seasonal bounds, and inter-modal covariance structures.
  - Current Operational Tier (e.g., 2026 operational months): Freshly ingested test observations evaluated against
    the historical baseline: Deviation = Observed_2026 - Climatological_Baseline_{cell, month}.
  - DO NOT pool operational test data with historical baseline training data, preventing lookahead leakage.
